## Viewing Tenants

The most important "contact in the TAHO CRM is the tenant. When
you want to find or add a tenant, you will go to the Tenants page. To do so, go
to [**Contacts > Tenants**](https://crm.lataho.org/civicrm/add/tenant) in the CiviCRM menu bar.

![contacts-tenants.jpg](/img/contacts-tenants.jpg)

> [!TIP] What is a contact?
> All of the people and organizations that interact
> with the TAHO program are registered as **contacts** in the CRM. This
> includes tenants, TAHO and LAHD staff, landlords and property managers, and
> even organizations like CBOs. Even the building a tenant lives in is
> registered as a contact. Through the CRM, we can also describe the
> **relationships** between these contacts. For example, we can say that
> Landlord A is the landlord of Tenant B, or that LAHD Investigator C is
> assigned to investigate Tenant D's harassment case

The Tenants page looks like this:

![tenants-screen.jpg](/img/contacts-tenants.jpg)

At the top, there is a search box that allows you to search for a tenant. You
can search using the tenant's **name**, **phone number**, or **email address**.

Searching will filter the table at the bottom of the screen.

### Adding a new tenant

If you need to add a new tenant, you can do so by clicking the button labeled
**Create New Tenant**.

> [!IMPORTANT] Before you add a new tenant, make sure they are not already in
> the system. This helps to prevent duplicates. Search for the tenant using
> their name, phone number, and email address. When you are sure that they are
> not already in the system, you can add a new one.

Clicking **Create New Tenant** will pop up a window like this:

![quick-add-tenant](/img/quick-add-tenant.jpg)

Fill out all the information you have about this tenant. Their name and phone
number are the most important information. Any other information you have is
helpful. The demographic information at the bottom at the form has been
requested by LAHD. If you know this information, please enter it.

When you have finished filling out the form, click the button labeled
**Submit**. This will return you to the Tenants page. You will see the tenant
you just added at the top of the table.

![tenants-page-demo](/img/tenants-page-demo.jpg)

### Tenants table

The table at the bottom of the Tenants page shows the tenant's **name**, **phone
number**, the name of their **household**, the number of open TAHO **cases**
the tenant has, and buttons/menus that allow you to perform specific actions
for the tenant.

![tenants-page-table](/img/tenants-page-table.jpg)

## Households

As you can see, almost all of the tenants in the list have a **Household**. A
household is a group of tenants that reside in the same home. When you add a
new tenant to the TAHO CRM, you will need to add them to a household. It's
common for TAHO staff to work with multiple members of a family who are
undergoing harassment together. Each person that we interact is added as their
own **tenant** in the CRM. We use the household to group these tenants together
so that we can see that they live together and are subjected as a group to the
landlord's harassment.

### Adding a Tenant to a Household

If a tenant has no yet been added to a household, you will see the warning "No
household" and a button labeled "Add Household"

![tenant-no-hh](/img/tenant-no-hh.jpg)

To add the tenant to a household, click "Add Household" button, which will pop
up a window that looks like this:

![add-hh](/img/add-hh.jpg)

This page will create a **relationship** between the tenant and the household
that they are a member of. It has two menus: the first is for the tenant, and
the second is for the household that the tenant will be added to. The tenant
menu will already be filled in because you clicked on that tenant's row in the
table on the Tenants page.

Click the menu labeled **Find or create household**. If you know that this
tenant has an existing family member or roommate in TAHO, you can search for
the family member's name, email address, or phone number to find their
household.

When you find the household you are looking for, select it, then hit the
**Submit** button to link the tenant to the household.

After you click **Submit**, you will see that the tenant's row now shows the
household, and there are two new buttons in the Actions column.

![tenant-list-hh-added](/img/tenant-list-hh-added.jpg)

### Creating a new household

If you know that this tenant does not yet have a TAHO household, you
can create a new one by clicking **Quick Add Household**.

![hh-search](/img/hh-search.jpg)

Another page will pop up that allows you to enter the information for the
household. Usually, you will want to inherit the address, phone number, and
email address for the household from one of the tenants that are in the
household. By default, it selects the tenant that you are adding to the
household, but you can use the menu labeled **Tenant** to pick a different
tenant.

![add-hh-form](/img/add-hh-form.jpg)

Checking the boxes for **Address**, **Email**, and **Phone** will copy those
values from the tenant to the household. This prevents you from having to
re-type the information into the household after having already entered it for
the tenant.

![hh-num-children](/img/hh-num-children.jpg)

At the bottom of the form, there is a field for the number of children in the
household. If you know this information, please enter it. It has been requested
by LAHD.

Finally, click **Save** to create the household. Then, click **Submit** to link
the household you just created to the tenant.

## Tenant Cases

A **case** is where we track our work supporting a tenant (or household of
tenants) with TAHO. The tenants table lists the number of cases that the
household has and allows you to see the list of their cases. The vast majority
of tenants will have only one case. You can also create a case for a tenant
from this page.

### Create case

Once a tenant has a household, you will see two new buttons: **Create case
directly** and **Assign case intake**. All tenant navigation actions are
logged within the case. For more information about cases and how to create
them, proceed to the next guide.
