## Updating Your Contact Information

The first time you log into the TAHO CRM, you should visit the contact profile
for yourself and update the contact information.

Click the triangular icon in the top menu bar of the CRM, and the click **View
My Contact**

![view-my-contact](/docs/img/view-my-contact.jpg)

This will take you to your contact profile. Click the **Edit** button, and fill
out the phone number field with the phone number that you use for work. Also
make sure that your email address is correct.

If you want to add your work address, you can do that too, but it isn't
necessary.
