## Reaching the TAHO CRM

Reach the TAHO CRM at <https://crm.lataho.org/civicrm>

If you're not logged in, you will see a screen that looks like this:

![login-screen](/docs/img/login-screen.jpg)

If you're new to TAHO, make sure the data manager (Philip:
<philip@inter-modal.com>) knows. He will create a new user account for you.

## Logging In

Your username will be your work email address. If you're logging in for the
first time, click **Reset your password** to generate a password reset to your
email address. Then, you can create your own password and return to this page
to log in.

## Accessing the TAHO CRM Home Screen

When you first log in, you will see a page that looks like this:

![drupal-homescreen](/docs/img/drupal-homescreen.jpg)

To click the button that says **CiviCRM** in the bar at the top of the screen.

> [!TIP] Tip You can also just go directly to the URL
> <https://crm.lataho.org/civicrm>. It's a good idea to bookmark this URL so
> that you can get back to it easily in the future.
