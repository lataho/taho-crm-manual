## What's a building

A building is a property where tenants live. Building is a case role, which
allows us to see a list of cases that are associated with a given building.
Building is also linked to **Property Agent** contacts via **landlord**,
**owner**, and **property manager** relationships. This allows us to keep track
of who owns and manages a building. This, in turn, allows us to count the
number of TAHO cases that exist against a given landlord. It also means that we
can guide our outreach efforts by targeting buildings owned by landlords known
to be likely to harass tenants.

> [!TIP] Important
> Having accurate building records with all of the essential information filled out (APN, RSO/JCO status, Council District) is important. This information is essential for our ability to understand where harassment is occurring and who is responsible.

## Buildings page

Just like with tenants, you can view all of the Buildings that we have created
through **Contacts > Buildings** in the menu bar. Or just go to
<https://crm.lataho.org/civicrm/search/buildings>.

You'll see a screen that looks a lot like the tenants page: a search field at
the top, and a table at the bottom.

The table displays the essential information about each building. You can click
to edit the data in the table.

## Creating a building

To create a new building, click the **Add Building** button.

> [!TIP] Important
> Make sure the building doesn't already exist by searching for it in the search field!

Give the building a name. The best practice is to use the street address as the
name. If the building has an additional well-known name, include it in
parentheses.

Add the building's address. Then, go to [ZIMAS](https://zimas.lacity.org) and
look up the building to get the APN, Council District, RSO (Rent Stabilization
Ordinance) status, and JCO (Just Cause Ordinace) status.

![zimas](/docs/img/zimas.jpg)

Here's where to find each piece of information on ZIMAS:

| Info             | Section                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------- |
| APN              | Address/Legal Information  |
| Council District | Jurisdictional Information |
| RSO              | Housing                    |
| JCO              | Housing                    |

> [!IMPORTANT] Important
> The building is the same thing as the _parcel_ or the
> _property_. It is _not_ the tenant's individual unit. Your building address
> should **not** include a unit number.

After you have entered the building information, click **Use this Building** to
create the building and add it to the case.
