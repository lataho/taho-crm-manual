The **Log Evidence** activity allows you to record each time you support a
tenant with gathering evidence of harassment. Logging evidence is important,
because LAHD wants to know how often support tenants with evidence-gathering,
and they want to know what types of harassment we have observed. Logging
evidence activities allows us to report to LAHD accurately. It also helps us
help the tenant, because the evidence log can be used when you are helping the
tenant write a demand letter and file a complaint with LAHD.

### How to log evidence

From the tenant's case page, click the **Log Activity** button, then, under **General activities**, click **Log Evidence**.

![log-evidence](/docs/img/log-evidence.jpg)

### When to log evidence

Any time that you are learning about harassment experienced by the tenant, you should create a Log Evidence activity.

> [!TIP] Important
> You should create a Log Evidence activity each **time** that you help the tenant gather evidence. So, if you call the tenant or meet with them in person and gather 10 different **pieces** of evidence (e.g. ten photos of code violations), that is still just **one** evidence activity. You can and should add lots of information to the one evidence activity to make sure that everything you gathered is captured. This matters because we report to LAHD on how many **instances** of evidence-gathering support we provided to the tenant. So if you log multiple activities for one meeting with the tenant, then we are double-counting.

## Evidence-gathering guidelines

When logging evidence, please follow these guidelines:

- In the date field, use the date that you are gathering the evidence, not the
  date of the harassment
- Use the details field to include notes and other information. Be as specific
  as possible. Include the date and time of the harassment in your notes. The
  more info, the better.
- Include all of the types of harassment that apply. You can select multiple
  types of harassment
- In the **Link** field, add a URL to a folder containing digital files,
  images, videos, or other media.
- If you provided disability accommodations (ADA assistance?) or
  interpretation/language support, please mark accordingly.

## Storing Evidence

The CRM does not allow you to upload documents or other media. (Trust me, you
wouldn't want that!) Instead, you should use your CBO's cloud storage provider
(e.g. Google Drive).

How each CBO organizes its evidence files is up to the CBO, but it is important
to keep things organized. It helps us and the tenant to be diligent about this.
